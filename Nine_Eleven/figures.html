Main Figures

The September 11, 2001 attacks involved several groups of people. The attacks were carried out by 19 hijackers who were members of the terrorist organization al-Qaeda, led at the time by Osama bin Laden. They hijacked four passenger airplanes. The people affected included passengers and crew members on the planes, workers and visitors in the World Trade Center and the Pentagon, and many firefighters, police officers, and emergency workers who responded to the attacks. Thousands of people were killed or injured.

Osama Bin Laden

Osama bin Laden was the founder and leader of al-Qaeda, the terrorist organization responsible for the September 11 attacks. He helped organize and support the plan and became the main public figure associated with the attacks.


Khalid Sheikh Mohammed

Khalid Sheikh Mohammed is considered the main planner of the 9/11 attacks. He developed the idea of using hijacked airplanes as weapons and presented the plan to Osama bin Laden.


Mohammed Atta

Mohammed Atta was one of the 19 hijackers and was the leader of the group that carried out the attacks. He was also the hijacker-pilot of American Airlines Flight 11, which crashed into the North Tower of the World Trade Center.


Marwan al-Shehhi

Marwan al-Shehhi was another hijacker-pilot. He flew United Airlines Flight 175 into the South Tower of the World Trade Center.


Hani Hanjour

Hani Hanjour was the hijacker-pilot of American Airlines Flight 77, which crashed into the Pentagon.


Ziad Jarrah

Ziad Jarrah was the hijacker-pilot of United Airlines Flight 93. That plane crashed in Pennsylvania after passengers and crew members tried to take control of the aircraft.


In total, 19 al-Qaeda hijackers took part in the attacks. They were divided among four airplanes, and each group had a pilot and other hijackers who helped take control of the plane.
